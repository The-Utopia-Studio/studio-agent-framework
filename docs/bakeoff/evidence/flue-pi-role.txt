reference/data-persistence-api states the invariant explicitly:
  "The stream is the sole authoritative transcript - canonical state is reconstructed
   by replaying it from the beginning, and an adapter must not model a second
   transcript in session rows, snapshots, or event streams."

DEPENDENCY SKEW (D-row): two copies of pi-ai in the tree.
  top-level @earendil-works/pi-ai@0.84.3  (installed for createProvider)
  @flue/runtime -> @earendil-works/pi-ai@0.83.0  (bundled)
  The custom provider is constructed with 0.84.3 objects and consumed by a runtime
  built against 0.83.0. It worked here; it is an untested compatibility surface.
